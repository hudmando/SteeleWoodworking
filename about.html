Steele Woodworking is a small studio focused on custom quality furniture and architectural woodwork — pieces meant to last decades, not seasons.

The maker

I've been working with wood for most of my life. both my great grandfathers were Oregon loggers, both my grandpas worked in the woods or in mills, and my dad was an arborist. I grew up making wooden swords with my brother in the same woodshop I make furniture in today. As I aged both my attention and skill towards the art of woodworking grew until I found myself where I am today.

The shop

Most of my work is done in my grandpas workshop using wood my dad fell and dried. truly a family buisness. My work is achieved with a mixture of modern and timeless tools and methods. tablesaws and handsaws, planers and chisels. The philosophy that both me and the wood are in agreement with the work, with the sculpting, both giving everything to see the final result emerge.

Working together

Whether big or small I see my work as part of my life and part of my servitude to God. If you are interested in working together on a project and seeing wood come to life check out the create tab.
