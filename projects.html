About | Create | Projects

The one thing that connects these pieces is the wood. Most, if not all, of the wood comes from a lumber yard filled by years of sawyering through my father’s tree business. It’s a joy to create furniture out of lumber taken from the backyards of our neighborhood. Another connection is the obvious growth in skill, a development that leaves its mark on each piece. It’s a wonder to see.

Notes on the work

A funny paradox of woodworking is that even after the tree is initially cut down. Once given new life in the form of furniture the wood ends up living its life mostly in one place, just as it spent all its tree life in one place. Being blown by the wind, it now is rocked gently by human touch and use. All to say, wood is a human companion. It always has been. I try my best to reflect the elasticity yet firmness that trees so wonderfully embody in my work.
